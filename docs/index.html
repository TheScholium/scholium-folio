Saving it
  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Build, then Save as PDF. In the print window choose Save as PDF; leave margins at default.
  3. Copy the PDF into the Document folder on your Supernote (USB, Partner app or cloud).
Nothing leaves your computer: the Folio is built in this page.
